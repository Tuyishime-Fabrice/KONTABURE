How it works

Your entire boutique. One connected system.

Products, stock, sales, customer credit and reports, together in one place. Here's how a shop runs on KONTABURE, from the first product you add to the report you read at closing.

01First

Set up your shop

Add your products, organise your categories and record your starting stock.

  • Start from ready-made product templates
  • Add up to 300 items in one go
  • Categories and units ready from day one

02Second

Manage your inventory

Record supplier deliveries, keep stock levels right, and adjust when the shelf doesn't match.

  • Record every delivery against its supplier, so stock and buying costs stay right
  • Count the shelves and fix the differences
  • See every stock movement, with who made it

03Third

Record daily sales

Ring up what customers buy in seconds, and how they pay.

  • Every sale saved with its items
  • Payments tracked by method, branch and cashier
  • See which products sell best

04Fourth

Manage customer credit

Sell on credit to your regulars, see who owes the shop, and record repayments as they come in.

  • Know exactly who owes you, and how much
  • Record repayments and see balances update
  • Set credit limits for each customer group

05Finally

Understand your business

Open the dashboard and reports to see how the shop is really doing.

  • Daily, weekly and monthly reports
  • Sales, stock movements and best sellers
  • AI insights that explain what changed, and why
